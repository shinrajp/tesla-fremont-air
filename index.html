How readings are shown

Map numbers are raw PurpleAir readings (the same as the PurpleAir map’s raw view). Each sensor card also shows an EPA-corrected AQI, an official adjustment that often reads a bit lower. Alerts and group-post thresholds use the raw 1-hour value. 10-minute short bursts are flagged for review only and do not by themselves trigger a group post.

WHO 2021 guideline (reference only): 15 µg/m³ 24-hour, 5 µg/m³ annual. Sources: PurpleAir.com public sensors; U.S. EPA PM2.5 AQI; wind from Open-Meteo. This page does not claim the facility caused a given reading.
